# CineStream Product Requirements

## Overview
A premium movie and TV show streaming platform designed for high performance and immersive viewing experiences.

## Core Screens
1. **Home (Desktop & Mobile)**: Hero carousel with trending content, personalized "For You" rows, categories, and a sleek navigation bar.
2. **TV Shows (Desktop & Mobile)**: Dedicated landing for shows, filtering by genre/network, and "continue watching" sections.
3. **Movies (Desktop & Mobile)**: Grid of movies with hover effects for trailers, advanced sorting (IMDb rating, release date), and featured collections.
4. **Series / Detail View (Desktop & Mobile)**: Immersive background art, episode list with thumbnails, cast information, and "more like this" recommendations.
5. **Account (Desktop & Mobile)**: Profile management, billing, viewing history, and download settings in a clean, organized layout.

## Design Constraints
- **Theme**: Dark Cinematic (High contrast, deep blacks).
- **Interactions**: Smooth transitions, glassmorphism cards, responsive grids.
- **Performance**: Minimalist UI elements to prioritize content (posters/trailers).
